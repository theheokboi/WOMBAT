## Inputs

- Canonical facilities provide authoritative point geometry.
- `country_mask` defines the coverage domain.
- Fixed-resolution `country_mask` metadata may override the configured adaptive base resolution.

## Decision Flow

1. Build the country coverage domain from `country_mask`.
2. Convert facilities into H3 assignments at each working resolution.
3. Count facilities per cell inside the coverage domain.
4. Recursively split occupied cells until the facility floor or density stopping rules are satisfied.
5. Recursively split empty cells when hierarchy, boundary-band, near-occupied, or minimum-output rules require it.
6. Refine coarse neighbors until the configured max neighbor resolution delta is satisfied.
7. Compact eligible sibling groups when that preserves the existing topology and smoothing guarantees.
8. Filter final output against country geometry before publishing cells.

## Occupied-Cell Rules

- Occupied cells always refine until at least `facility_floor_resolution`.
- Occupied cells may continue refining until `facility_max_resolution`.
- Occupied cells split beyond the floor when `facility_count > target_facilities_per_leaf`.

## Empty-Cell Rules

- Empty cells refine until they satisfy the hierarchy below the effective base resolution.
- Empty cells also refine until `min_output_resolution`.
- Empty cells near the boundary band or near occupied cells may refine up to `facility_floor_resolution - 1`.
- Empty interior cells cap at `empty_interior_max_resolution`.

## Neighbor Smoothing

- v3 enforces `max_neighbor_resolution_delta` as a hard output guarantee.
- The smoothing pass refines the coarser side of an offending adjacency when allowed.
- Empty cells may refine past their normal interior cap when smoothing requires it.

## Post-Compaction

- v3 merges fully covered sibling groups back to their parent when the merge remains inside the domain and preserves neighbor-delta guarantees.
- Empty near-occupied compaction is gated by `compact_empty_near_occupied`.
- Boundary-band empties are never compacted.

## Output

Published cells preserve the stable adaptive schema:

- `h3`
- `resolution`
- `layer_value`
- `layer_id`
- `asof_date`

Published metadata includes:

- `layer_name`
- `layer_version`
- `policy_name`
- `coverage_domain`
- `params`
- `stopping_rules`
- additive `adaptive_counters`
