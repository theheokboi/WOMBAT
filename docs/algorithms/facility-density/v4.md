## Intent

- Preserve the recursive split flow from v3.
- Keep low-density areas coarse and dense clusters fine.
- Let sparse occupied branches stop earlier than v3.
- Remove the v3 post-compaction exception that allowed occupied singleton regions to collapse below their chosen occupied resolution.

## Inputs

- Canonical facilities provide authoritative point geometry.
- `country_mask` defines the coverage domain.
- Fixed-resolution `country_mask` metadata may still override the configured adaptive base resolution for this version.
- `facility_floor_offset` controls how far below `facility_floor_resolution` sparse and topology-refined branches may stop.

## Decision Flow

1. Build the country coverage domain from `country_mask`.
2. Convert facilities into H3 assignments at each working resolution.
3. Count facilities per cell inside the coverage domain.
4. Recursively split occupied cells until the v4 sparse-floor or density stopping rules are satisfied.
5. Recursively split empty cells when hierarchy, boundary-band, near-occupied, or minimum-output rules require it.
6. Refine coarse neighbors until the configured max neighbor resolution delta is satisfied.
7. Compact eligible empty sibling groups when that preserves the existing topology and smoothing guarantees.
8. Filter final output against country geometry before publishing cells.

## Occupied-Cell Rules

- Sparse occupied branches refine until `max(min_output_resolution, facility_floor_resolution - facility_floor_offset)`.
- Here, "sparse" means `facility_count <= target_facilities_per_leaf`.
- Sparse occupied branches stop there when `facility_count <= target_facilities_per_leaf`.
- Dense occupied branches continue refining while `facility_count > target_facilities_per_leaf`, up to `facility_max_resolution`.
- Post-compaction does not merge occupied sibling groups back to a coarser parent.
- Isolated singleton cells can stop one level earlier than the old v3 occupied floor, while clusters still recurse deeper when counts stay above target.
- Neighbor smoothing may still refine a sparse branch past that earlier stop if it touches a much denser branch.

## Empty-Cell Rules

- Empty cells refine until they satisfy the hierarchy below the effective base resolution.
- Empty cells also refine until `min_output_resolution`.
- Empty cells near the boundary band or near occupied cells may refine up to `max(min_output_resolution, facility_floor_resolution - facility_floor_offset)`.
- Empty interior cells cap at `empty_interior_max_resolution`.
- Empty near-occupied compaction remains gated by `compact_empty_near_occupied`.

## Neighbor Smoothing

- v4 enforces `max_neighbor_resolution_delta` as a hard output guarantee.
- The smoothing pass refines the coarser side of an offending adjacency when allowed.
- Empty cells may refine past their normal interior cap when smoothing requires it.

## Post-Compaction

- v4 only compacts empty sibling groups.
- Boundary-band empties are never compacted.
- Empty near-occupied compaction remains optional.

## Output

Published cells preserve the stable adaptive schema:

- `h3`
- `resolution`
- `layer_value`
- `layer_id`
- `asof_date`

Published metadata includes:

- `layer_name`
- `layer_version`
- `policy_name`
- `coverage_domain`
- `params`
- `stopping_rules`
- additive `adaptive_counters`
